## Tree unimodality search

Every tree through order 26 has log-concave domination coefficients. The check combines exact counting with a proof for trees whose bare vertices interact in groups of at most two. The unrestricted tree unimodality conjecture remains open.

## Domination roots

Domination roots of trees of diameter six are dense in the complex plane. Six is sharp. Trees of diameter at most five have a common zero-free region. This rules out a uniform zero-free neighborhood of the positive axis as a route to tree unimodality.
